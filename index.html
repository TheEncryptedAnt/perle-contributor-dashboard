What is Perle?

Perle is a platform where users complete structured AI tasks through official website quests.

Step 1 — Wallet Setup

Visit official website, connect your Solana wallet and ensure small SOL balance for transaction fees.

Step 2 — Marketplace

Open Marketplace section and select an active quest.

Step 3 — Follow Instructions

Read instructions carefully before starting. Accuracy matters.

Step 4 — Submit & Verify

Complete task properly and check Earnings / Badge section for confirmation.
